Our services

Everything the land asks of your project.

Five integrated practice areas covering the full environmental lifecycle — assess, approve, monitor, close out. Appoint one team and every discipline talks to the others.

01 / Practice area

Environmental

The core of what we do: taking projects through Botswana's environmental authorisation process and keeping them compliant once ground is broken. We prepare assessments regulators can approve and management plans site teams can actually implement.

  • Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA) — screening, scoping, specialist studies and full EIA reports for authorisation.
  • Environmental Management Plans (EMP) — practical, construction-ready plans with clear responsibilities.
  • Environmental audits & compliance monitoring — scheduled and independent audits against your EMP, permits and conditions.
  • Environmental monitoring & reporting — dust, noise, waste and water monitoring with regulator-ready reports.
  • Risk assessments — environmental risk identification, ranking and mitigation planning.
  • Waste & pollution management — waste plans, spill response and pollution prevention.
  • Soil treatment & contaminated land management — assessment, remediation planning and verification.
02 / Practice area

Archaeology & Heritage

Botswana's Monuments and Relics Act requires heritage clearance before development. We survey, assess and monitor so your programme is never stopped by an unexpected find — and when one appears, your team knows exactly what to do.

  • Archaeological Impact Assessments (AIA) — statutory assessments for development consent.
  • Archaeological surveys & monitoring — pre-construction surveys and watching briefs during earthworks.
  • Heritage assessments — built heritage, graves and sites of cultural significance.
  • Chance-find procedures — clear, site-ready protocols embedded into your EMP.
  • Archaeological induction & awareness — training for contractors and site personnel.
03 / Practice area

Ecology & Biodiversity

From the Kalahari to the Okavango's fringes, we document what lives on your site before, during and after development — protecting sensitive species, satisfying lender biodiversity standards and proving rehabilitation actually worked.

  • Ecological & biodiversity assessments — baseline ecology for EIAs and lender due diligence.
  • Flora & fauna surveys — seasonal species surveys by discipline specialists.
  • Vegetation monitoring — fixed-plot and transect monitoring over the project life.
  • Wildlife & sensitive species monitoring — protected and red-listed species management.
  • Biodiversity management — offset, avoidance and habitat management planning.
  • Rehabilitation monitoring — verifying re-vegetation and habitat recovery to close-out.
04 / Practice area

Water & Natural Resources

In a semi-arid country, water makes or breaks a project. We assess the resource, monitor its quality and keep your abstraction and discharge inside the rules — protecting both the aquifer and your licence to operate.

  • Water resource assessments — surface and groundwater availability and sustainable yield.
  • Water quality monitoring — sampling programmes, laboratory coordination and trend reporting.
  • Groundwater & hydrogeological support — borehole siting, monitoring networks and aquifer protection.
  • Water pollution assessments — source identification, plume assessment and remediation advice.
  • Water management & compliance — water use licensing support and compliance reporting.
05 / Practice area

Planning, Compliance & SHE

The discipline that keeps everything else honest: safety, health and environment on the ground, every day. We inspect, train and report so incidents fall, contractors comply and communities stay onside.

  • Environmental & SHE inspections — routine and unannounced site inspections with actionable findings.
  • Risk assessments & JSAs — task-level job safety analyses and project risk registers.
  • Contractor compliance monitoring — holding contractors to your EMP, permits and SHE plan.
  • Incident & corrective action management — investigation, root cause and close-out tracking.
  • SHE training — inductions, toolbox talks and awareness campaigns.
  • Community & stakeholder engagement — consultation, grievance mechanisms and disclosure.
  • Property condition surveys — pre- and post-construction dilapidation surveys.
